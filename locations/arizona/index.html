Frequently Asked Questions

What areas in Arizona do you serve?

We serve 17 cities across Maricopa County in the greater Phoenix metropolitan area — including Scottsdale, Paradise Valley, Phoenix, Chandler, Gilbert, Mesa, Tempe, and the west valley communities.

How does the Arizona desert climate affect cleaning?

Desert dust settles on indoor surfaces, and hard water leaves buildup on bathroom fixtures and shower glass. Our recurring checklist covers surface dusting, bathroom cleaning and floor care; deep cleaning adds further detail. Pool tile is outside these residential checklists.

Do you clean luxury homes and estates in Scottsdale?

Yes. Estate cleaning is one of our core services in the Scottsdale and Paradise Valley market. We handle homes with specialty surfaces including travertine, onyx, natural stone, and premium appliances from Sub-Zero, Wolf, and Miele.
