Is This Right for You?

Who it’s for

Owners of luxury residences, historic homes, and properties with high-value finishes and collections. Property managers overseeing estate portfolios. Homeowners with specific surface care requirements that standard cleaning services cannot meet.

  • Your home is 5,000+ square feet and standard cleaners don't have the equipment or staffing to do it properly
  • You have marble, natural stone, hardwood, antiques, or specialty surfaces that require surface-specific products
  • Household staff coordination or property manager involvement is part of how your home operates
  • Discretion matters — you need a team that signs a confidentiality agreement and doesn't ask questions
  • Previous cleaners have left residue on stone, streaks on custom millwork, or the wrong product on hardwood

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

What's included in an estate clean that isn't in a standard deep clean?

Estate cleaning follows our deep-clean checklist, with cleaning products selected for your home’s materials and finishes. It includes cabinet exteriors, baseboards, doors, windowsills, shower-grout scrubbing and accessible cleaning beneath furniture. Refrigerator, oven and dishwasher interiors, interior window glass and tracks, and wet-wiping blinds are paid add-ons.

How do you handle confidentiality?

We sign a confidentiality agreement before the first visit. Our teams are briefed on privacy expectations — no photos, no conversations about the property or its contents outside of the team, and coordination only through the contact you designate.

Who has access to my estate?

Every Salt & Slate cleaning professional is licensed, background-checked, and fully insured. We brief every team on privacy expectations and conduct before the first visit, and the same detailed checklist and 24-hour satisfaction guarantee apply on every cleaning.
