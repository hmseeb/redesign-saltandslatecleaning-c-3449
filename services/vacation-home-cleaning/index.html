A vacation home should be ready when you arrive. Plan cleaning before your next stay, after a visit or between longer absences so dust and everyday mess do not become your first job on arrival.

Our vacation-home checklist covers kitchens, bathrooms, bedrooms and living areas. We clean appliance exteriors and the microwave interior, dust surfaces, clean floors, change sheets and replace towels using the fresh linens you provide.

The visit ends with a property check: faucets off, toilets not running, doors locked as required and the thermostat at the agreed setting. Refrigerator, oven and dishwasher interiors, laundry, interior window glass and tracks, and wet-wiping blinds are paid add-ons. If you also host paying guests, we can arrange Airbnb turnovers under that service’s checklist.

Is This Right for You?

Who it’s for

Owners of mountain homes, desert retreats, and lake properties who want their vacation home maintained and guest-ready year-round. Also serves owners who rent their properties part-time and need reliable turnover between personal use and guest stays.

  • Your property sits empty between stays and you want cleaning scheduled before you return
  • You arrive to your vacation home and spend the first afternoon cleaning instead of unwinding
  • The property gets rented part of the year and you need a team that handles both owner-stay prep and rental turnover
  • Dust has built up between visits and you want kitchens, bathrooms, bedrooms and living areas ready for your arrival
  • You need lockbox or smart lock access coordination from a team you can trust remotely

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How do you access my property if I'm not there?

We coordinate lockbox or smart-lock access before the appointment. The checklist includes a final property check: faucets off, toilets not running, doors locked as required and the thermostat returned to the agreed setting. We also capture post-clean quality photos.

Can you handle both owner-stay prep and rental turnover for the same property?

Yes. Tell us whether the visit prepares the home for you or for paying guests. We confirm the appropriate service checklist and extras in your quote. Vacation-home cleaning and Airbnb turnover have different appliance and laundry inclusions, so we agree on those before scheduling.

How often do you recommend cleaning an empty vacation home?

Plan cleaning around your visits and the amount of dust that builds up while you are away. Share how long the home has been empty and its current condition so we can recommend vacation-home cleaning or a deep clean.
