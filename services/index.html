Cleaning services for your home

  • Standard & Recurring CleaningWeekly, biweekly, or monthly premium home cleaning. Consistent teams who learn your home's unique needs.→
  • Deep CleaningDetailed home cleaning with baseboards, cabinet exteriors, doors, windowsills and shower-grout scrubbing. Refrigerator, oven and dishwasher interiors, and interior window glass and tracks, are paid add-ons.→
  • Move-In CleaningPrepare your next home before unpacking. Our checklist covers empty cabinets, drawers and closets, refrigerator and oven interiors, kitchens, bathrooms and floors. Interior window glass and tracks are paid add-ons. Homes with construction dust need post-construction cleaning.→
  • Move-Out CleaningLease-end and pre-sale cleaning with empty cabinet, drawer and closet interiors, refrigerator and oven interiors, kitchens, bathrooms and floors. Clear belongings and storage spaces before the visit, and select any paid add-ons when booking.→
  • Vacation Home CleaningCleaning before your next stay or between visits to your mountain, desert or lake home. Kitchens, bathrooms, floors, fresh linen changes and a final property check, with access arranged while you are away.→

  • Estate CleaningDetailed cleaning for larger residences using our deep-clean checklist and products suited to your home’s materials and finishes.→
  • Specialty CleaningExpert care for natural stone, marble, granite, hardwood, wine cellars, home gyms, radiant heated floors, and other premium surfaces.→
  • Post-Construction CleaningDetailed final cleaning after construction or renovation: loose construction dust, cabinet interiors, trim, window tracks, glass doors and floors, once the trades have finished and cleared the space.→
The Salt & Slate Difference

A higher standard of clean

Salt & Slate operates as a licensed and fully insured cleaning company. Every cleaning professional passes a comprehensive background check before stepping foot in your home, and every visit follows the same detailed room-by-room checklist customized to your space.

Each service has a room-by-room checklist showing what is included and which tasks are available as add-ons. Our 24-hour satisfaction guarantee covers the work on your booked checklist.

Background-Checked Professionals

Every cleaning professional undergoes a thorough background check before stepping foot in your home. We verify identity, criminal history, and references so you never have to wonder who has access to your space.

Fully Insured

Complete liability coverage on every job. Your home, your belongings, and your peace of mind are protected from the moment we arrive until we leave.

Same Standard Every Visit

A detailed room-by-room checklist on every cleaning. Your home's specific notes — preferred products, surfaces to avoid, areas needing extra attention — are attached to your account and applied every time.

Satisfaction Guaranteed

If any detail falls short, we return to address it within 24 hours at no additional cost. No fine print, no pushback, no exceptions.
