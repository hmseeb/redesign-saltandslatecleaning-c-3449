Is This Right for You?

Who it’s for

Homeowners, general contractors, project managers and commercial property owners preparing completed construction or renovation projects for occupancy.

  • Homeowners preparing a completed new build, renovation or addition for move-in
  • General contractors and project managers arranging the final clean after trades and contractor cleanup are complete
  • Owners of vacant or partially furnished properties with construction dust in the agreed cleaning areas
  • Sellers preparing a completed project for photos, showings or handover
Commercial Projects

Commercial facilities and warehouses

We perform the same final clean on commercial projects: office and medical tenant improvements, retail and restaurant buildouts, ground-up commercial construction, and new or renovated warehouses and distribution facilities. Once every trade has cleared the site, our team captures construction dust from reachable ledges, door frames, window systems, and fixtures with HEPA-filtered shop vacuums, details restrooms and breakrooms, cleans interior glass doors and shower glass, and finishes hard floors from private offices to open warehouse slab.

We schedule around your turnover or occupancy date and coordinate directly with your general contractor, project manager, or facility manager. A certificate of insurance is available for GC prequalification on request.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How is post-construction cleaning different from a move-in cleaning?

Post-construction cleaning focuses on fine drywall and sanding dust across accessible surfaces, cabinet interiors, window tracks and trim. It takes more detailed vacuuming and wiping than a move-in clean. Our final clean starts once construction and contractor cleanup are complete.

Do you remove paint splatter, mortar, drywall mud or adhesives?

These specialty removals are not included. Paint splatter and overspray, mortar, cement, dried grout and grout haze, dried drywall mud or plaster, adhesives, excess caulk, protective films and contractor repairs should be handled by your contractor before our final clean. Loose construction dust is included.

Does the property need to be empty?

We clean vacant and partially furnished properties. Tell us which areas need cleaning and which will be accessible. Cabinets, drawers and appliances must be empty for interior cleaning. Your quote identifies the agreed areas and appliance work.

When should I schedule the final clean?

Schedule after the trades have finished and tools, materials, floor protection and bulk debris have been removed. Have contractor residue removal and finish corrections completed first. Water and electricity must be available. Send project details and photos so we can confirm the scope and timing.

Why does post-construction cleaning take more time?

Even an empty, newly built space can require substantial cleaning time. Fine dust collects inside drawers, along edges and throughout detailed finishes. Removing it can take repeated passes across many individual surfaces. Your quote reflects the size, affected areas, condition and agreed work. Specialty residue removal remains excluded from that work.

Why can dust return, and is a follow-up visit included?

Fine construction dust may settle again after even a thorough cleaning. Any follow-up visit to remove newly settled dust is not included in your original quote and will be quoted separately for your approval. For the best result, finish all dust-producing work before your appointment, have your HVAC filters checked and replaced as needed, and keep contractors out of cleaned areas.

Do you bring air scrubbers or clean inside ductwork?

Our final clean uses HEPA-filtered shop vacuums and surface-cleaning products. Air-scrubber service and internal duct cleaning are not part of the final-clean scope. Have your HVAC professional check the system and filters after construction.
