Is This Right for You?

Who it’s for

Busy families, working professionals, and anyone who wants their home to stay consistently clean without dedicating their own time to it. Recurring cleaning is our most popular service because it eliminates the cycle of mess and marathon cleaning sessions.

  • Your home has already had a deep clean and you want to keep it at that level without doing it yourself

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Salt & Slate a maid service?

Salt & Slate provides what most people mean when they search for a maid service or housekeeper — scheduled house cleaning on a weekly, biweekly, or monthly basis, performed by licensed, background-checked, and insured professionals. The difference is service tier. Every visit follows the same detailed room-by-room checklist, uses commercial-grade products, and is backed by our 24-hour satisfaction guarantee. Pricing is by home size, not by the hour, so the scope is fixed before any work begins.

Do I have to sign a contract?

No contracts. You can adjust your frequency, pause for travel, or end recurring service anytime — there is no fee for ending your plan. For individual appointments, reschedules and cancellations with at least 48 hours notice are free. With less notice, a $50 minimum cancellation and rescheduling fee applies; same-day cancellations are billed per our cancellation policy.

Do I need a deep clean first before starting recurring service?

Yes. Before we put you on a recurring schedule, your home needs to be at maintenance condition. That means a deep clean first — either booked through us or recent enough that we can confirm the baseline when we arrive. Recurring cleaning is designed to maintain a clean home, not catch it up from a long stretch. If a deep clean isn't in your budget right now, we offer hourly blocks at $50 per cleaner with a $150 minimum, though those visits don't carry a checklist guarantee or our satisfaction guarantee.

Who cleans my home?

Every Salt & Slate cleaning professional is licensed, background-checked, and fully insured. The same detailed room-by-room checklist applies on every visit — so the standard, the coverage, and the 24-hour satisfaction guarantee remain consistent regardless of which cleaner is on the job.

What does a recurring clean actually include vs. a deep clean?

Recurring cleaning covers countertops, appliance exteriors, the microwave inside and out, sinks, toilets, showers, floors, mirrors, fan dusting and blind dusting. It includes kitchen cabinet spot-cleaning and bathroom cabinet wiping. Deep cleaning adds full kitchen cabinet-front wiping, baseboards, doors and door frames, windowsill wiping and shower-grout scrubbing. Refrigerator, oven and dishwasher interiors, interior window glass and tracks, and wet-wiping blinds are paid add-ons to either service.
