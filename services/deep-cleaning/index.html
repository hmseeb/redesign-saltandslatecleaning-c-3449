Residential Services — Deep Cleaning

A detailed clean for a fresh start

Detailed home cleaning with baseboards, cabinet exteriors, doors, windowsills and shower-grout scrubbing. Refrigerator, oven and dishwasher interiors, and interior window glass and tracks, are paid add-ons. Salt & Slate provides deep cleaning, rated 5.0 out of 5 on Google · 39 Google reviews.

A deep clean gives extra attention to the details that routine visits maintain around: oven hood vents, cabinet fronts, baseboards, doors, window frames, windowsills and shower grout. It is a useful reset before guests arrive, after a busy stretch or before starting recurring cleaning.

We wipe cabinet and drawer exteriors, detail baseboards and door frames, scrub shower grout and vacuum beneath furniture where reachable. We do not move furniture. The room-by-room checklist below defines the work included in your visit.

Microwave interior cleaning is included. Refrigerator, oven and dishwasher interiors, interior window glass and tracks, wet-wiping blinds, and sliding-door glass and tracks are paid add-ons. For empty cabinet, drawer, pantry and closet interiors, compare move-in or move-out cleaning.

Is This Right for You?

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Why do I need a deep clean before starting recurring service?

Recurring cleaning keeps a clean home clean. It isn't built to scrub grout, degrease hood vents, or detail every baseboard — it's built to maintain a home that's already at that level. If we put a recurring crew into a home that hasn't been deep cleaned, the detail work gets skipped, the results are inconsistent, and you're not getting what you're paying for. The deep clean sets the baseline. Recurring keeps it there.

What's NOT included in a deep clean?

Refrigerator, oven and dishwasher interiors, interior window glass and tracks, wet-wiping blinds, and sliding-door glass and tracks are paid add-ons. Cabinet, drawer, pantry and closet interiors are outside the deep-clean checklist. Microwave interior cleaning is included.

What if I don't want to start with a deep clean?

No worries. We have hourly blocks available at $50 per hour per cleaner with a minimum of $150. We can target your top priority areas during that time. Please note that hourly cleans do not come with our satisfaction guarantee, and we cannot guarantee everything on your priority list will be finished within those hours.

Can I book a deep clean as a one-time visit without starting recurring service?

Yes. Book a single deep clean for guest preparation, a seasonal refresh or a home that needs more detailed attention. You do not need to start a recurring plan. Choose post-construction cleaning for a property with construction dust.

What is the main difference between a deep clean and a standard clean?

A deep clean adds baseboards, cabinet and drawer exteriors, interior doors and door frames, windowsills and window frames, accessible cleaning beneath furniture, and shower-grout scrubbing to routine cleaning. Move-in and move-out cleaning add empty cabinet, drawer, pantry and closet interiors, plus the refrigerator and oven interiors. The microwave interior is included in all three service types.
