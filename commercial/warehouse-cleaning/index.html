Warehouse Cleaning Across Utah, Arizona, Nevada & Texas

Salt & Slate provides commercial cleaning for warehouses, distribution centers, fulfillment facilities, and manufacturing support areas across Utah, Arizona, Nevada, and Texas. Service covers warehouse floors, loading docks, office areas, break rooms, and restrooms — scoped to your facility's specific checklist.

Licensed, background-checked, and fully insured cleaning professionals follow your facility's scope of work and any safety requirements you specify. Scheduling fits your shift pattern — between shifts, third shift, or weekends. Every visit is backed by our 24-hour satisfaction guarantee.

Scope of Service

Plan Your Cleaning Scope

Your quote confirms the areas, tasks and frequency for your facility.

  • Concrete and epoxy warehouse floors
  • Loading docks and shipping/receiving areas
  • Racking and shelving (surface dust removal)
  • Cobweb and dust removal from accessible surfaces
  • Office areas within the warehouse
  • Break rooms, kitchens, and vending areas
  • Restrooms
  • Staging and pack zones
  • Exterior dock areas and entry points
  • Accessible lighting fixtures and vent covers
Protocols

  • 24-hour satisfaction guarantee on every cleaning
  • Month-to-month service — no long-term contracts required
  • Certificate of insurance provided on request for facility prequalification

Common Questions

Warehouse Cleaning — FAQ

Does Salt & Slate handle warehouse cleaning?

Yes. Salt & Slate provides commercial cleaning for warehouses, distribution centers, fulfillment facilities, and manufacturing support areas — sometimes listed as industrial janitorial in facility contracts. Cleaners are licensed, background-checked, and fully insured. Service follows your facility's checklist and safety requirements on every visit, and scheduling fits your shift pattern.

What does warehouse cleaning include?

We clean warehouse floors, loading docks, shipping/receiving areas, office areas within the warehouse, break rooms, and restrooms. High-reach cobweb and dust removal is included. Scope is set by your facility's requirements. We do not add to or modify your scope without approval from your facility manager.

What commitments come with the service?

Licensed and insured cleaners on every visit. 24-hour satisfaction guarantee — if anything is missed, we return. Month-to-month service with no long-term contracts. Certificate of insurance provided on request.
